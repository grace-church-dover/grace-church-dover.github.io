Sermons Give

Our goal in providing biblical counseling is to care for you by helping you meet life's challenges in a way that ultimately glorifies the Lord Jesus Christ and allows you to fully enjoy His love for you and His plans for your life. We believe that the Bible provides guidance and instruction for issues of faith and life. Therefore, our counseling is based on timeless scriptural principles rather than secular psychology or psychiatry. We have various trained biblical counselors among us that are willing to come alongside you during your time of need.

If you have questions or would like to learn more, please contact John Smith.
