We are a multi-generational church with women from various ages and stages of life. Our Women's Ministry aims to create opportunities for genuine friendships and pathways for intentional discipleship, as we seek to pass along spiritual truth from one generation to the next (Titus 2). Toward this end, we are comitted to study and teach the Scripture faithfully, love one another deeply, and engage in truthful conversations as we grow in spiritual maturity together. We seek to provide a variety of studies and events throughout the year for our ladies to engage in.
